Exercise guide

  • Chair squats: sit back toward a sturdy chair and stand up slowly.
  • Wall push-ups: hands on wall, body straight, bend and press away.
  • Glute bridges: lie on your back, knees bent, gently lift hips.
  • Bird-dogs: on hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg with control.
  • Dead bugs: on your back, lower opposite arm and leg slowly if comfortable.

Start with 1–2 comfortable rounds and rest when needed. Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness or unusual shortness of breath.

🍲 Practical Indian meal ideas

Flexible ideas, not a strict diet. Eat enough to feel satisfied; adjust portions to your hunger and needs.

Breakfast

Idli or dosa with sambar and egg; or oats with milk, nuts and fruit.

Lunch

Rice or chapati with chicken, fish, egg or dal, plus vegetables and curd.

Snack

Fruit, roasted chana, yogurt, milk, nuts or a boiled egg.

Dinner

Chapati or rice with fish/chicken/egg/dal and vegetables.

Drink water regularly. No food needs to be labelled “bad,” and you do not need to skip meals or count every calorie.
